# **Our Story**

Dear Margeaux began with a simple belief: that beautiful, well-crafted bags shouldn't be reserved for the few.

## The Beginning

It started with a sketch on a napkin in a small Parisian café. What if we could create bags that captured the timeless elegance of French design while remaining accessible to the modern woman? That question became our mission.

## Our Philosophy

Every piece in the Dear Margeaux collection is designed with intention and made with care. We believe that luxury isn't about exclusivity—it's about quality, craftsmanship, and the joy of owning something truly beautiful.

### Quality Materials

We source the finest Italian leather, selecting each hide for its supple texture and natural character. Our brass hardware is designed to age gracefully, developing a patina that tells the story of your journey with each bag.

### Thoughtful Design

Our designs draw inspiration from the understated elegance of French fashion. Clean lines, balanced proportions, and versatile silhouettes ensure that each piece complements your style for years to come.

### Limited Quantities

We release our collections in limited drops, ensuring that each bag receives the attention it deserves during production. This approach allows us to maintain exceptional quality while creating pieces that feel special and personal.

## The Name

Margeaux is more than a name—it's an homage to the classic French tradition of craftsmanship. The name evokes images of sun-drenched leather workshops, artisans bent over their work, and the satisfying click of a well-made clasp.

## Our Commitment

We're committed to creating bags that you'll reach for day after day. Bags that grow more beautiful with time. Bags that become part of your story.

Welcome to Dear Margeaux. We're so glad you're here.

*— The Dear Margeaux Team*
