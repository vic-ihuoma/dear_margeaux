# The Debut

A visual journey through our inaugural collection.

Welcome to Dear Margeaux's first collection—a celebration of timeless elegance and exceptional craftsmanship. Each piece in The Debut embodies our commitment to creating luxury bags that transcend trends and become cherished companions.

## The Vision

The Debut collection was born from a simple yet powerful vision: to create handbags that whisper rather than shout, that complement rather than compete. Drawing inspiration from the effortless sophistication of French style, we've crafted seven distinctive pieces, each with its own personality yet united by our dedication to quality.

## The Collection

From The Colette's refined elegance to The Vivienne's bold confidence, every bag in this inaugural collection tells a story. The names—Amélie, Giselle, Margot, Eloise, Céline—evoke the timeless grace of women who know that true luxury lies in the details.

## Our Promise

With The Debut, we introduce not just a collection, but a philosophy. Each bag is crafted with the finest materials, designed to age beautifully, and made in limited quantities to ensure exclusivity. This is just the beginning of our journey together.
