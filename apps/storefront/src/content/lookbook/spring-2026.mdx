# Spring 2026 Collection

A celebration of new beginnings and timeless elegance.

Our Spring 2026 collection draws inspiration from the golden warmth of Mediterranean mornings. Each piece is crafted with the finest Italian leather in colors that capture the season's optimistic spirit.

## The Inspiration

This collection was born from lazy afternoons spent wandering through sun-drenched markets, where the soft glow of aged terracotta meets the crisp whites of linen. We wanted to create bags that carry not just your essentials, but the feeling of a perfect spring day.

## Featured Pieces

From the structured elegance of the Siena Tote to the casual charm of the Positano Crossbody, each piece in this collection is designed to be your companion through every springtime adventure.
